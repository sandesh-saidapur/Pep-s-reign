# Findings v1: shot-based view of City, 2015/16 to 2024/25

Source: openfootball results, Football-Data.co.uk shots and shots on target (all 3,420 scores cross-checked and agreeing). Computed by `pep-reign tables` on 25 Nov 2026. No xG yet: Understat is in maintenance.

Average points per game: 1.74 in the baseline season, 2.30 across Pep's nine seasons.

## Against the league average (shots per game, season average = 0)

| Measure | Baseline (2015/16) | Pep era average |
|---|---|---|
| Shots for | +3.25 | +4.69 |
| Shots against | -4.01 | -5.34 |
| Shots on target for | +1.27 | +1.98 |

Positive shots-for is good, and negative shots-against is good, so City were better than the league on both in both eras, and the gap widened under Pep.

## Rankings

- City had the **fewest shots conceded per game in the league in every season** from 2015/16 to 2024/25. Checked against the raw table.
- City ranked 1st or 2nd for shots created in every Pep season except 2016/17 (3rd) and 2022/23 (3rd).
- In 2024/25 City still conceded the fewest shots, but the number rose to 9.3 per game, its highest in the period.

## Caveats

- **Shots are not chances.** Shot counts ignore quality. xG is the next step, and it depends on Understat.
- Football-Data's shot counts come from its own data provider and definitions, which we haven't verified against another source.
- One club, nine seasons, one league: patterns here are descriptive, not causal.
- Points per game and league position are based on the simplified table in `pep-reign` (no head-to-head tie-breaks). They match the published final tables for the seasons I checked from memory. Please verify against premierleague.com before publishing.
